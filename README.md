# TIGER
TIGER resting-state, diffusion MRI, and quantitative MRI preprocessing scripts

TIGER organizational/analysis scripts for the following projects:

Ho et al. "Default Mode and Salience Network Alterations in Suicidal and Non-Suicidal Self-Injurious Thoughts and Behaviors in Adolescents with Depression" (published in Translational Psychiatry, 2021)

Ho et al. "Higher levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines are associated with higher levels of glutamate in the anterior cingulate cortex in depressed adolescents." (published in Frontiers in Psychiatry, 2021)

Liu et al. "Functional and Structural Brain Connectivity of Default Mode, Central Executive, and Attentional Regions Predict Longitudinal Trajectories of Depression and Anxiety Symptoms in Adolescents with Major Depressive Disorder"

qT1_analysis_HoSisk2021.Rmd refers to analytic pipeline used in Ho & Sisk et al. "Sex differences in myelin content of white matter tracts in adolescents with depression" (published in Neuropsychopharmacology, 2022)
